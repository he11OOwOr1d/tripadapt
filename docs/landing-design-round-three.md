# Additional design exploration — 8 November 2026

Local-only visual samples, preserving prior samples and product contracts.

## Design plans

- Window: plum #54334E, pink mist #F6EDF2, rose dust #C9A9B9, light #FFFAFD. DM Sans and Lora. An arch acts as a window into travel; scene choice updates it. Left-aligned copy and generous margins.
- Fieldnote: forest #23463B, celadon #DCE8DB, chalk #F7FAF4, leaf #8EA77C. Urbanist. Oversized photographic field and overlapping route; reorder stops through explicit controls. Natural imagery supplies detail.
- Departure: indigo #34365B, lilac #EDECF8, white #FAFAFF, muted violet #8E8BA7. Sora. Boarding-pass geometry introduces an actual connected trip; expandable logistics keep feature detail discoverable.
- Afterhours: midnight #182A42, pale blue #D8E5F1, dusk #8196B0, slate #263C56. Lora plus DM Sans. Night photography and ample stillness, with a pace slider changing a sample afternoon. No neon accents.
- Somewhere: cobalt #2543B4, butter #F5E9A4, white #FCFBF5, blue mist #E1E8FF. Outfit. Bold destination chooser feels like a small exploration game; selections change the image and invitation.

Layout comparison:
Window: navigation / text beside arch / scene choices / itinerary rewrite / closing.
Fieldnote: navigation / photo with large title / route workbench / budget/stay details / closing.
Departure: title above wide ticket / expandable logistics / disruption preview / closing.
Afterhours: night panorama / slow-pace day planner / saved inspiration / closing.
Somewhere: giant type beside cutout photo / choice-driven destination reveal / adaptable day / closing.

## Brief critique

These must vary structure and interaction, not just palette. Window's serif is justified by a quiet framed-view concept, distinct from cream/terracotta. Departure's ticket is a storytelling metaphor, explicitly not a purchased reservation. Afterhours uses dark blue for night atmosphere, not a generic black/neon theme. Somewhere spends boldness in one typographic choice-driven hero. No fabricated reviews, customer statistics, or claims of live travel data. Controls remain keyboard operable and motion is action-led/reduced-motion aware.

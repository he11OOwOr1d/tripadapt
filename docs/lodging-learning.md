# Learning: recording where you stay, honestly

19 November 2026. Each destination with at least one night now has a **Where you're staying** panel in the day view. The traveler records a name, an optional address or note, check-in and check-out times, and whether they have booked it themselves. They can type a name or use **Find stays nearby**, which lists up to 12 mapped hotels, guesthouses, hostels, apartments, motels and chalets within 5 km of the destination centre. A stay with a location shows as a blue house-shaped pin on the day map.

## What it is and is not

It is the traveler's own record. It is **not** a booking, a price, an availability check or a review, and nothing contacts a supplier. The wording says so: "marked as booked by you", "Prices, availability and booking status are not checked". A name-only stay is valid; it just has no map pin. The map search is a partial OpenStreetMap category search, not a list of every hotel, and many OSM names are short or generic (the live Jaipur search returned names such as "Fly" and "Jaipur").

## Decisions and tradeoffs

- **No migration.** The record is an optional `lodging` object inside the existing per-stay entry in `preferences.stay_preferences`, keyed by the stop's logical id. That path is already validated, copied between plan versions, frozen in reviews and returned by the API, so the feature inherits version, ownership and autosave behaviour without a new table. The cost: it is not queryable by SQL and not a normalized ledger. If stays later need dates per night, several stays in one destination or linked costs, that is the point to promote it to a table.
- **Reuse the place search.** `lodging` is a sixth category in the existing activity discovery (Geoapify `accommodation`, or OSM `tourism=hotel|guest_house|hostel|apartment|motel|chalet` for the Overpass fallback), so it keeps the same caching, rate limits, retention and attribution. The activity pickers list their categories explicitly, so lodging never appears as an activity.
- **A searched place keeps its source.** The stay stores the provider id, coordinates and source text. Editing the name of a searched stay drops the location unless the name is unchanged, so a renamed place never keeps the old pin.
- **Validation twice.** The backend `Lodging` model and the frontend `isLodging` agree: name 1-120 plain characters, address up to 200, `HH:MM` times, both coordinates or neither, (0, 0) rejected, a provider id requires coordinates, unknown fields rejected. The frontend also accepts the `null`s the server returns for unset fields.
- **Not part of the visit line.** The stay pin is unnumbered and is never joined by the dashed line; the numbered line stays the visits. It does widen the map bounds so it is never off screen.
- **An import cycle.** `day-map.ts` takes only a type from `lodging.ts`, because `lodging.ts` imports `validCoordinates` from it and the unit-test loader resolves imports recursively.

## From the stay to the first visit (20 November 2026)

A recorded stay with a map location can now answer the first question of the day: when do I have to leave? Under the stay panel, a **Getting from your stay to <first visit>** disclosure takes a mode (walk or drive without live traffic) and an arrival buffer, calls `POST /api/v1/journeys/from-stay` and shows "Leave by HH:MM to reach <visit> at HH:MM" with the minutes, distance, route warnings and the Geoapify/OpenStreetMap credit.

- **A separate endpoint, not a loosened one.** `/journeys/check` takes two retained place ids and a gap between two activities; a stay is neither a retained place nor does it have an end time. The new request takes the stay's coordinates (range-checked, (0, 0) rejected, NaN/inf rejected), a retained destination place id, mode, local start time and buffer. It returns the leave-by time, or `needs_earlier_start` when the visit starts too early for the route (leave-by would be before midnight). Same Geoapify route call, rate limit and `private, no-store`; nothing is stored.
- **First visit** is the earliest non-skipped activity of the day. Both ends need coordinates; unmapped visits or stays say so and never get a name-geocoded guess. Changing the stay, mode, buffer, or the visit's time/place discards the result.
- **Verification.** Automated: 12 new backend tests (leave-by arithmetic including the midnight boundary, 7 invalid inputs, unknown place 404, sign-in 401, no database writes) and 3 frontend tests; 561 backend and 204 frontend tests pass; typecheck and build pass; OpenAPI snapshot regenerated. Live, signed in (local web and API, hosted database, QA trip `77d6c6b1-ec94-4653-ba83-2d4fa8d41101`): with ITC Rajputana as the stay and Amrapali Museum at 10:00, walking returned "Leave by 09:24" (26 min + 10 min buffer, 1.6 km) and driving "Leave by 09:44" (6 min, 4.1 km); changing the mode cleared the old result; at 390 px there was no overflow and no control under 44 px. The QA trip was returned to its baseline (visit removed, stay removed) and a reload confirmed it.
- **Several visits (live, signed in, same QA trip):** with a stay and four visits (two at 10:00, two at 13:00) the check named the earliest visit, breaking the 10:00 tie by id exactly as the timeline does; the map showed the blue stay pin plus numbered visits 1-4 with the status "4 mapped visits shown; your recorded stay is marked". All four visits and the stay were then removed and a reload confirmed the baseline. This closes the earlier gap about a stay beside numbered visits.
- **Not verified:** a screenshot (the preview window was hidden for most of the run), the hosted app (not pushed), and a first visit before 00:30.

### The way back (20 November 2026, later)

The same section now also answers the evening question: from the day's **last** visit (latest end, skipped ones ignored) back to the stay. `POST /api/v1/journeys/to-stay` takes the stay coordinates, a retained origin place, mode and the visit's end time, and returns the arrival time ("Leaving Jaipur Wax Museum at 18:00, you would be back at your stay by 19:20") or `after_midnight` when arrival would pass 24:00. A visit that itself runs past midnight has no end clock time, so the UI says to split it instead of calling the API. No buffer applies on the way back. Seconds in the end time round up. Results for the two legs sit side by side; changing the mode or buffer clears both.

### The AI day planner knows the stay (20 November 2026, later)

The Groq day planner now receives the recorded stay for the day's destination as plain evidence: name, address, check-in/out and whether it has a map location, never the raw coordinates. When the stay has a location, each mapped candidate also carries `distance_from_stay_km`, a straight-line distance rounded to 0.1 km (computed server-side from retained place records), and the prompt tells the model to prefer nearer candidates, leave time to get back, and never claim or confirm a booking. The stay text is treated as untrusted, like every traveler label. Validation is unchanged: the model can still add only catalog ids, the extra key is ignored by the checks, and the accept boundary rebuilds the catalog without it.

- **Straight-line, not a route.** The distance is a hint, labeled so in the prompt; real journey times still come from the explicit checks.
- **Why not coordinates?** The model does not need them and they are the traveler's lodging location; distances give it what it needs.
- **Verification.** Automated: 4 new backend tests (stay appears in the model context without coordinates; absent without a stay; distances only for a located stay, about 10 km for a 0.09 degree shift; the stay helper returns None without a stay) and a full run of 574 passing. ### Check-in and check-out caveats on the route checks (20 November 2026, later)

The stay route checks assume the traveler starts the day at the stay and ends it there. That is not true on the arrival day before check-in or on the departure day after check-out, so the section now says so instead of presenting a confident "leave by". On the arrival day, when the first visit starts before the check-in time (or the last visit ends at or before it), it adds: "Check-in is from 14:00, so you may not be at your stay yet. This estimate assumes you start from it." (or "return to it"). On the departure day, when a visit starts at or after the check-out time: "You check out at 11:00, before this visit starts, so you may have left your stay by then. ..." Days in between, and stays without those times, show nothing. The estimate itself is not changed or hidden; the note only qualifies it. The rule is a pure function compared as zero-padded HH:MM strings.

Verified: 1 new frontend test covering every branch (208 pass); build passes. ### The stay as "where I am" in the fresh-start check (20 November 2026, later)

The fresh-start readiness check asks "Where are you now?" and offers only mapped places already on the day. A hotel you searched for was not among them, even though "I'm back at the hotel" is the most common place to restart from. A stay chosen from the nearby-stays search now keeps the id of its retained place record (`place_id`), and the readiness context adds that place to "Mapped places in this plan".

- **No new trust.** The id only nominates a candidate. The context query still keeps only place records that exist and belong to the stop's destination reference, so a stale or foreign id is silently left out, and the later check validates the chosen place exactly as before. Retained place rows are immutable in the database, so they cannot vanish.
- **Rules.** `place_id` must be a UUID and needs the searched place (`external_id`) it came from; a name-only stay has none. Editing a searched stay's text keeps it while the name is unchanged and renaming drops it, like the coordinates.
- **Compatibility.** Stays saved before this change have no place id and stay valid; the server now returns `place_id: null` for them, and the frontend validator accepts that. Deploy the backend first, as before, so it accepts the field before the frontend sends it.
- **Verification.** Automated: 2 backend model tests and 1 context test (the stay is listed; an id the server does not hold is not) and 1 frontend test (accepts a UUID, null and name-only; rejects a non-UUID, a number, and an id without a searched place); 582 backend and 209 frontend tests pass, OpenAPI regenerated, build passes. Live, signed in, on the QA trip: after saving ITC Rajputana from the search, "Check a fresh start for today" with the saved draft listed "ITC Rajputana" under "Mapped places in this plan", and choosing it gave "I'm at ITC Rajputana at the selected time". The stay was removed and a reload confirmed the baseline and the session.
- **Not verified:** a full readiness check and proposal started from the stay (the QA trip has no remaining activities, so the check would stop at "no flexible remainder"), the delay flow (it takes its location differently and was not changed), and the hosted app.

### Booked stays as commitments, and dates from the trip (20 November 2026, later)

The PRD's accommodation acceptance says a booked stay is a commitment and a change should be flagged for the traveler to act on, and that each stay has its own check-in and check-out dates. Two additions, both the traveler's own numbers, nothing checked with a supplier:

- **Nights you booked.** When "I have booked this myself" is ticked, an optional "Nights you booked" field appears (1 to 365; blank is fine, other values are refused with a message). It is stored on the stay record (`booked_nights`, no migration) and shown ("marked as booked by you for 3 nights"). When the plan's nights for that destination later differ, the panel says: "You marked this stay as booked for 3 nights, but your plan now has 2 nights here. Check your booking, or update the nights you booked." It compares only those two numbers, never flags a stay with no nights entered or one that is not marked booked, and clears as soon as the numbers agree.
- **Dates from the trip.** When the trip has exact dates, the panel adds "From your trip dates: check-in Fri, Dec 4, check-out Sun, Dec 6", the destination's arrival day and departure day, with the entered times, and says the dates follow the trip and should be confirmed with the booking. With flexible or open dates it shows nothing rather than guessing.

Not built: several stays within one destination (the PRD allows them; each destination still has one stay record), and a flag when the booked check-in time conflicts with a recorded arrival.

### Changing the nights of a booked stay (20 November 2026, later)

"Change nights between stays" already protected fixed, booked, completed and skipped *activities*, but ignored a stay the traveler had marked as booked, so a booked 4-night stay could be shortened to 3 without a word. The preview now adds, for each booked stay whose nights the change alters, a clearly marked line: "Check your booking. You marked your stay "Haveli QA" in Jaipur as booked for 2 nights, and this change moves its nights from 2 to 1. It does not block the change (the traveler may be changing it on purpose, and the app cannot cancel anything), it only makes the commitment visible before they apply. A stay not marked booked, or whose nights do not change, is never listed.

- **Compatibility.** The new `booked_stays` field is optional on the frontend, so a frontend that gets a reply from an older server still works, and the backend adds a key to a dictionary the older frontend ignores.

## Several stays in one destination, phase 1 (20 November 2026)

The PRD (FR-04) says travelers can have multiple stays, each with its own dates. A destination can now have its main stay plus up to four others, each covering its own nights (for example nights 1 to 2 at one place and 3 to 4 at another), and the panel offers **Add another stay in <destination>**.

- **Data.** `Lodging` gains optional `first_night` and `last_night` (1-based, inclusive; both or neither; first no later than last) and the stay entry gains `extra_lodgings` (at most four). A stay without a range covers every night no other stay claims, which keeps every existing single stay valid and unchanged. The backend refuses another stay without nights and any two stays that share a night; the frontend validator and the panel enforce the same, so a plan that the server would reject cannot be saved from the page. No migration (it lives in the existing stay preferences JSON).
- **Panel.** The same panel is reused for every stay (title "Another stay", required first and last night, "Nights are counted in this destination: 1 to N. Two stays cannot share a night."). It checks the range is within the destination's nights and names a clash ("Another stay already covers night 2."). The main stay offers an optional range once other stays exist. Each stay shows its own nights, its own dates (derived from the trip dates: the main stay's dates shrink to the nights it still covers) and its own booked-nights check (its nights, not the destination's).
- **Coverage notes.** When some stays have ranges, the page says plainly which nights have no stay ("No stay is recorded for nights 1-2.") or that a stay is set beyond the plan's nights; it stays quiet when the main stay covers the rest.
- **Chat.** The route and day answers name every stay with its nights ("staying at Main Haveli (marked as booked by you); then Hostel QA (night 2); your own record, not confirmed with the stay"), built server-side as before; a single stay keeps its earlier wording.

**Not done in phase 1:** the day map, the leave-by and way-back checks, the AI planner, the fresh-start check and the restore review still use the main stay only; they do not yet pick the stay for a given night.

### Keeping a stay fixed (20 November 2026)

PRD FR-07 separates **locking** from **booking**: an item marked "keep fixed" must not be changed by automatic replanning unless the traveler unlocks it, and a lock "does not make an activity a confirmed booking". Activities had that; stays did not. Each stay now has **Keep this stay fixed** (a `locked` flag on the stay record, no migration), independent of "I have booked this myself", with the text "A fixed stay keeps its nights: "Change nights between stays" will not change them until you untick this. It is not a booking and nothing is reserved." The summary says "kept fixed".

The only automatic change to a stay's nights is "Change nights between stays", so that is where the lock bites: if the change would alter a destination that has a locked stay (main or other), it is refused before anything is computed, with 'Your stay "Fixed Haveli" in Jaipur is kept fixed, so its nights are not changed automatically. Unlock it in the stay panel first, or choose other stays. Nothing changed.' A change that leaves the locked stay's destination alone is unaffected, and unlocking lets the same change through. Booked and locked are independent: a booked stay is warned about, a fixed one is protected, and a stay can be either, both or neither.

Verification: backend tests for the default, for lock not implying booked, and for the rebalance (blocked with the stay's and destination's names, allowed when another destination changes, allowed once unlocked; 640 backend tests) and a frontend test for the validator and summary (249 frontend tests); typecheck and build. Live, signed in, on the disposable trip: with a fixed stay, previewing "remove 1 night" showed "This needs your decision" with the message above and nothing changed; after unticking the lock the same preview came back ("Jaipur: 2 to 1 nights"), I cancelled without applying, axe found no violations, and I removed the stay. Not done: locking a journey (the PRD also lists transfers), which no automatic change touches today.

### A confirmation reference on a stay (20 November 2026)

PRD FR-07 asks for optional external confirmation references on committed items, "without requiring document uploads". Journeys had one; stays did not. A stay now has an optional **Confirmation number or note** (up to 100 plain characters, `reference` on the stay record, no migration), shown in the summary ("marked as booked by you, reference Conf 88/12") and prefilled when editing. It is only what the traveler typed: never checked, never required, nothing uploaded, and the same plain-text rule as the rest of the record (no control characters). It is not sent to the AI or the chat.

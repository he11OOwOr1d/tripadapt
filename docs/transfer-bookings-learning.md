# Learning: recording a journey you already booked

20 November 2026. The PRD (FR-05) asks for an "Add existing transport booking" form: the traveler enters the mode, endpoints, departure and arrival times and an optional reference for a journey booked elsewhere, so the itinerary reserves that time. Until now the arrival and departure cards only held a mode, an approximate number of hours and an allowance. Each card now has **Add a journey I already booked**, and the day view flags activities that clash with the times entered.

## What it is and is not

It records what the traveler tells the app: an optional departure time (local to the journey's start), an optional arrival time (local to its end), an optional reference or note up to 100 plain characters, and an "I have booked this myself" checkbox. It is **not** a booking, a ticket check or a live status, and the text says so in three places: the form, the card summary ("As you entered it; not checked with the supplier") and the flags ("your recorded arrival"). Nothing contacts a supplier.

## What changes in the day view

- **Arrival counts on the first day at a stop.** An activity that starts before the recorded arrival is flagged: "Starts before your recorded arrival at 10:00."
- **Departure counts on the last day.** An activity that ends after the recorded departure is flagged: "Runs past your recorded departure at 09:30." A stop with no nights has one day, so both checks apply to it.
- **Existing behavior is untouched.** The flags use the same highlight and the same "Open a highlighted item" flow as overlaps; skipped activities never clash; no times means no flag. The banner now says "Some timings need a look" because overlaps are no longer its only cause.
- **Buffers are the traveler's own, visible assumptions.** Two optional fields, "minutes to settle in after arriving" and "minutes to be there before departing" (0 to 240, blank means none), widen the margin: an activity that starts inside the settling time is flagged ("Starts inside the 60 minutes you allowed to settle in after arriving at 10:00."), and one that ends inside the pre-departure time is too. Nothing is assumed unless the traveler enters it, and the card summary repeats the values.

## Decisions and tradeoffs

- **Extend the existing per-journey entry.** The journey's preference already carries the mode, hours and allowance, keyed by the route leg, so the booking fields (`departure`, `arrival`, `reference`, `booked`) are four new optional fields on it, not a new table or a migration. Older plans without them stay valid (a backend test loads one), and saving a booking changes only those four fields.
- **Validation twice.** The backend model and the frontend validator agree: times are `HH:MM` or blank, the reference has no control characters and at most 100 characters, unknown fields are rejected by the backend.
- **A journey's two ends are separate local times, on the right days.** The departure is local to the origin and counts on the origin stop's last day; the arrival is local to the destination and counts on the destination's first day. That also covers an overnight train: leave 22:00 on the last day in Jaipur and arrive 06:30, which is the first day in Agra, and a journey that crosses time zones needs no conversion in the app. What is not modelled is a journey whose own duration the app would have to place on the calendar (for example a transfer that fills an entire day, or a first stop day that is not the arrival day); those stay as the traveler's "nights on the move" and approximate hours.
- **Where it shows.** The card summarizes the entry. The activity flags come from a pure function (`transferConflicts`) that finds each stop's inbound and outbound journeys through the same route legs as the rest of the app.

## Verification

- **Automated:** 4 backend test groups (defaults, a valid booking, ten invalid cases including bad times and control characters, a plan round trip and an older plan still loading) and 7 frontend tests (validation, the update touching only the booking fields, the summary, arrival and departure days, a one-day stop, a second stop using the legs between stops, and merging); 607 backend and 232 frontend tests, ruff, typecheck and build pass; OpenAPI regenerated.
- **Live, signed in** (the disposable three-destination QA trip): I added a 09:00 visit to Jaipur's day, recorded an arrival of 10:00 with a reference and "booked by me" on the arrival card and a departure of 09:30 on the moving-on card. Focus moved into the form's first field; the cards summarized the entries; the visit was flagged with both "Starts before your recorded arrival at 10:00." and "Runs past your recorded departure at 09:30."; the banner appeared; a reload kept everything. I then removed both entries and the visit. Axe found one existing color (the conflict message at 4.41:1, never visible before because no conflicts had been on screen), now darkened to #87602f, after which it reports nothing with the form open; at 390 px there was no overflow and no control under 44 px other than the checkbox inside its 44 px label.
- **Not verified:** the hosted app, a screenshot, a booking on the main QA trip, and a second time zone (no check across time zones, since none is converted).
- **Not done:** using the recorded times in the chat's day answers or the journey checks, and a transfer as a block in the day timeline. The AI planner uses them: see the section below; the chat's route summary lists them.

## The AI day planner respects them

Without this the AI planner would happily propose a 09:00 museum on a day the traveler arrives at 10:00. The planner now narrows its planning window with the traveler's own recorded times: on the first day at a stop the window starts at the recorded arrival (if later than 09:00), and on the last day it ends at the recorded departure (if earlier than 18:00). The narrowed window is passed to the model as `planning_window`, with the raw entries as `traveler_recorded_arrival` and `traveler_recorded_departure` (the prompt says they are the traveler's entries, not verified), and **it is enforced after the model answers**: an addition that starts before the window or ends after it is rejected with "keep added blocks inside 11:00-18:00" and the model gets its normal repair attempt, so the guarantee does not depend on the model following the prompt. The window logic is one pure function (`planning_window`) that reuses the same route-leg keys as the web client. A middle day, or a day with no recorded times, keeps 09:00-18:00.

- **Verification:** 3 backend tests (arrival narrows the first day and a clash is repaired on the second attempt, departure narrows only the last day and the context carries the entry only there, and no recorded times means no new context) with 610 backend tests passing. Live, signed in, with a real Groq call and a local worker on the disposable three-destination trip: with a recorded arrival of 11:00 on the first day, the AI proposal's blocks started at 11:15, 12:30, 13:45, 15:00 and 15:45 and its explanation said "fitting the 11:00-18:00 planning window". I discarded the proposal and removed the arrival.
- **Not verified:** the departure case live, whether the model's choices improve (the enforcement, not the model, is what the tests and this run establish), the hosted worker, and the chat's "add a break" path (it shares the day graph but I did not run it).

### Buffers (later the same day)

The PRD asks for boarding and check-in buffers to be visible assumptions, so each recorded journey now also takes the two optional buffers described above (`arrival_buffer` and `departure_buffer`, whole minutes from 0 to 240; zero is the default and means none is assumed). They feed the day-view flags and the AI planner's window (the window starts at arrival plus settling time and ends at departure minus the pre-departure time, still enforced after the model answers). Backend validation rejects negative, over-240 and non-numeric values; the form rejects them with "Enter a buffer from 0 to 240 minutes, or leave it blank."

Verified: backend tests for the new fields (valid, invalid, default zero) and for both buffer directions in the planner's window (616 backend tests pass), a frontend test for both flag messages, the exact margins and the summary (233 frontend tests pass), typecheck and build. Live, signed in, on the disposable trip: a 10:30 visit with a recorded arrival of 10:00 and a 60-minute settling time was flagged "Starts inside the 60 minutes you allowed to settle in after arriving at 10:00.", an out-of-range value (999) showed the error, the card summary read "arrives 10:00 · 60 min allowed to settle in", and axe found no violations with the form open. I removed the details and the visit afterwards. Not re-run live: the planner window with buffers (unit-tested only).

## Review follow-up (20 November 2026)

A code review of the cache, booking, planner-window and buffer commits found, and I fixed:

- **A focus and notice bug.** The booking component was keyed on the saved values, so saving remounted it: the "Saved as you entered it" notice vanished and keyboard focus was lost (the same class of bug as the stay form's). The key is now the journey only. Verified live: after save and after remove the notice shows ("Saved as you entered it. Nothing was booked or checked." / "Booking details removed.") and focus returns to the Edit or Add button.
- **A contradictory caveat.** The static line "Departure time and arrival buffer not set." stayed above a saved booking; it now shows only while no booking details exist (hidden after saving, back after removing, checked live).
- **An impossible planner window.** An arrival at 17:30 with a 60-minute settling time left an inverted window that would be sent to the model. When less than 15 minutes remain, the planner now skips the model and returns an honest empty proposal ("No usable time is left on this day: your recorded arrival and departure leave 18:30-18:00, so nothing was added."), with a test that also passes the publish-boundary check. The context now reuses the one window string.

Reviewed and left as designed:

- **Overnight journeys (corrected after a second look).** The reviewer worried that an overnight arrival would be applied to the wrong day. It is not: a recorded arrival counts on the destination's first day, which for an overnight journey is the morning after the departure, and the departure counts on the origin's last day, so "22:00 out, 06:30 in" flags the right days. The only real limit is a journey the app would have to spread across several calendar days, which stays in "nights on the move".
- **Cache hits skip the per-user quota.** That is the point of the intercity cache (a public road route costs the provider nothing to repeat); it does mean a caller can repeat cached routes without spending quota, which is harmless because they cost no provider call. The warnings stored with an entry are provider data and are empty for Geoapify, so nothing user-specific is shared. A second process has its own empty cache, so behavior can differ between deployments; this is stated in the release plan.

617 backend and 233 frontend tests pass; typecheck and build pass.

## The chat's route summary lists recorded journeys

"Summarize my route" now adds a "Journeys, as you entered them (not checked with any supplier)" section: for each journey where the traveler recorded anything, one line with the mode, the hours, the departure and arrival, "marked as booked by you" and the reference, for example "Delhi to Jaipur: departs 08:00, arrives 11:30, reference PNR 7". The text is built on the server from the saved plan, so the entries never go to the language model (the same rule as the stay), journeys with nothing recorded are left out, and at most twelve are listed. Verified: a backend test (nothing is listed without entries, an empty entry adds nothing, the line is exactly as entered; 618 backend tests pass) and live through the real chat on the disposable trip, which listed "Delhi, National Capital Territory of Delhi, India to Jaipur, Rajasthan, India: departs 08:00 arrives 11:30 reference PNR 7"; I then removed the details (the chat messages remain in that trip's history, which is not plan data).

## The timeline shows the journey, not "time to leave open"

With an arrival recorded, the day's timeline still offered the hours before it as "Unplanned time, 2h to leave open", which is wrong: the traveler is still on the way. Open gaps now split around the recorded journey. On a stop's first day the time before the recorded arrival shows as **On the move until your recorded arrival at 10:00** (and the "+" on the remaining gap starts at the arrival, so a new activity defaults to a sensible time); on its last day the time after the recorded departure shows as **On the move from your recorded departure at 17:00**. A one-day stop gets both. Only open gaps change: activities are never moved or hidden (a clash is flagged separately, as before), middle days and days without recorded times are untouched, and the added row is a plain, non-interactive line in italics.

Verified: one pure function (`splitForJourneys`) with a frontend test for arrival, departure, a middle day, activities in the day, a gap entirely after the arrival, a one-day stop, no recorded times and an unknown stop (239 frontend tests pass); typecheck and build. Live, signed in, on the disposable trip: before recording, "08:00-22:00 Unplanned time"; after an arrival of 10:00, "08:00-10:00 On the move until your recorded arrival at 10:00" then "10:00-22:00 Unplanned time", with the plan-the-gap button labelled for 10:00; axe found no violations; I removed the arrival and the timeline returned to one open gap. Not verified: the departure row live (unit-tested only), the hosted app, and a screenshot.

## Changing nights moves a booked journey's day (20 November 2026)

A journey the traveler marked as booked is tied to a calendar day, and "Change nights between stays" moves that day when the nights before it change. The preview now adds, for every booked journey that moves, a line such as: "Check your ticket. Your journey from Jaipur to Agra, marked as booked by you (departs 09:00, reference PNR QA), moves 1 night earlier with this change. Your booking is not changed or checked by this; contact the operator or update it yourself." It does not block anything (the traveler may mean it, and the app cannot change a ticket) and it lists only booked journeys: unbooked ones move without a flag, and a journey before the first stay, or one whose earlier stays do not change, is not listed. The shift is the change in the total nights of every stay before the journey; overnight "nights on the move" are the same before and after and cancel out.

- **Compatibility.** The new `booked_journeys` field is optional on the frontend and an extra key for an older frontend, like `booked_stays`.
- **Verification.** A backend test (a booked leg after the stay that shrinks moves by -1 with its departure and reference; the unbooked return and a booked leg before any stay are not listed; lengthening the trip lists the one booked leg that follows by +2; 641 backend tests) and a frontend validator test (optional, empty, zero or fractional shifts and wrong types refused; 250 frontend tests); typecheck and build. Live, signed in, on the disposable trip: with a booked Jaipur to Agra journey (departs 09:00, reference PNR QA), previewing "remove 1 night from Jaipur" showed the line above; I cancelled without applying, axe found no violations, and removed the booking details.
- **Not done:** locking a journey; nothing changes journeys automatically except this nights change, which now tells you.
